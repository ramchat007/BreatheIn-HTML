Inside Every Unit

BreathePure™ Technology

A four-stage air intelligence system, engineered to eliminate what you cannot see. No gimmicks. No compromises.

01

Capture

A 360° air intake draws the full room volume through the unit up to 5 times per hour, leaving no corner untouched.

02

Filter

True HEPA H13 filtration captures 99.97% of particles as small as 0.3 microns — including PM2.5, pollen, and bacteria.

03

Neutralise

An activated carbon layer absorbs VOCs, cooking odours, smoke, and formaldehyde. What passes through is chemically neutral.

04

Monitor

Real-time laser sensors track PM2.5, CO&sub2;, humidity, and temperature — displayed instantly on the unit and the app.
