Choosing & Sizing

Use the Find My Purifier tool — it weighs your room size, ceiling height, concerns and placement to recommend the right model. As a quick guide, Air Pro suits bedrooms (35-40 m²), Air Pro 1 medium rooms (50-55 m²), Air Pro 2 large living spaces (75-80 m²), and Air Pro Max large or commercial spaces (120-150 m²).

CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate) measures how quickly a purifier cleans the air in a specific room size. A higher CADR means faster purification, which is essential during peak pollution hours or in larger spaces.

Purifiers work best in enclosed spaces. While a larger unit like the Air Pro Max can cover open-plan living areas, walls and closed doors block airflow. For optimal health benefits, we recommend placing a correctly sized unit in the rooms where you spend the most time, such as bedrooms and the living room.

Filters & Maintenance

Generally, every 8 to 12 months depending on usage and local air quality. Your purifier and the Smart Life app will alert you automatically when it's time for a replacement.

We price our replacement filters fairly to ensure you can maintain clean air year-round. Prices start at ₹1,499. Subscribing to filter replacements can save you an additional 15%.

You can gently vacuum or wipe the outer pre-filter layer, but the inner HEPA and Carbon layers are strictly non-washable. Washing a HEPA filter destroys its microscopic fibers and voids its performance.
