8hrs To stable AQI

The challenge

Their 1,800 sq ft sea-facing apartment battled a double problem: coastal humidity that encouraged dust mites and mould, plus fine particulate drifting in from the arterial road below. Visible dust settled within hours of cleaning, and their youngest had recurring nighttime congestion.

The solution

After using the Find My Purifier tool, the family installed two Air Pro 2 units — one in the open-plan living area and one in the children's bedroom. The 7-in-1 filtration handled particulate and humidity together, while the built-in humidifier kept levels balanced through the dry AC season.

The result

Within eight hours of running, indoor AQI settled into the "Good" band and held there. Over a month, average PM2.5 dropped 94%. The family reports clearer mornings, less visible dust, and uninterrupted sleep for their children.

"My daughter used to wake up every night with a blocked nose. Within a week of installing Breathe In, that simply stopped. It's the quietest, most reassuring purchase we've made for our home."

— Priya & Rohan Mehta, Worli, Mumbai
